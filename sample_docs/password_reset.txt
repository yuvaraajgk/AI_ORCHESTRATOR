# Password Reset and Account Lockout

## Overview

Your company account provides access to email, VPN, business applications, and other internal systems. If you forget your password or your account becomes locked after multiple failed login attempts, you may temporarily lose access until the issue is resolved. Most password-related issues can be fixed using the self-service password reset portal or by contacting the IT Service Desk.

---

## Common Issues and Troubleshooting

### Issue: Forgotten Password

**Symptoms**

* Unable to remember your password.
* Login fails with **"Incorrect Password"** or **"Invalid Credentials."**

**Resolution**

1. Ensure Caps Lock is turned off.
2. Verify that you are entering the correct username.
3. Open the company password reset portal.
4. Complete the identity verification process using Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) or the required verification method.
5. Create a new password that meets the company's password requirements.
6. Wait up to 10 minutes for the new password to synchronize across all systems before logging in again.

---

### Issue: Account Locked

**Symptoms**

* "Your account has been locked."
* "Too many failed login attempts."

**Resolution**

1. Stop attempting to log in repeatedly.
2. Wait for the automatic unlock period if your organization supports it (typically 15–30 minutes).
3. If immediate access is required, contact the IT Service Desk and request an account unlock.
4. After your account is unlocked, log in using the correct password.
5. If you have forgotten your password, reset it before attempting another login.

---

### Issue: Password Changed but Login Still Fails

**Symptoms**

* Password reset completed successfully.
* Unable to log in to email, VPN, or company applications.

**Resolution**

1. Wait 5–10 minutes for password synchronization.
2. Close and reopen the application you are trying to access.
3. Sign out and sign back in.
4. Restart your computer.
5. If multiple applications still reject the new password after 15 minutes, contact the IT Service Desk.

---

### Issue: Continuous Password Prompts

**Symptoms**

* Outlook, Microsoft Teams, or another application repeatedly asks for your password.
* Login window appears multiple times.

**Resolution**

1. Enter your updated password.
2. Sign out of the application.
3. Sign back in using the new password.
4. Restart the application.
5. Restart your computer if the issue continues.
6. Contact IT if password prompts continue after restarting.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often should I change my password?**

Follow your organization's password policy. Some organizations require periodic password changes, while others only require changes when there is a security concern.

---

**Q: How long does a password reset take to become active?**

Most password changes become active immediately, but synchronization across all company systems may take up to 10 minutes.

---

**Q: What makes a strong password?**

A strong password includes uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Avoid using names, birthdays, or common words.

---

**Q: Can IT see my password?**

No. IT administrators cannot view your password. They can only assist with resetting it.

---

## Important Notes

* Never share your password with anyone, including coworkers or IT staff.
* Do not reuse passwords from personal accounts.
* Always approve MFA requests only if you initiated the login.
* If you receive unexpected MFA notifications, deny the request immediately and report it to the IT Service Desk.
* Use an approved password manager if your organization permits password managers.
* Change your password immediately if you suspect your account has been compromised.
